# L-W06 TrainTimeLimitNotResumable

**Level: warning.** A training step has a time limit and cannot continue when it is reached.

## What it checks

A step with `kind="train"` sets `walltime=` and says nothing about `resumable=`, and nothing in its code uses
`kaos_lib.checkpoint`. A step that sets `resumable=` (either way), sets `checkpoint_every=` or saves checkpoints is not
judged here.

## Why it matters

A step that reaches its time limit, or loses its machine, is ended. A resumable step runs again and continues from
its last checkpoint; any other step starts again from zero, so a long training can lose all its work.

## Example

```python
train = step("train", kind="train", walltime="12h", produces=[model("model")])
```

## How to fix

Add `resumable=True` and save checkpoints with `kaos_lib.checkpoint` (see [Long jobs](../long-jobs.md)).

```python
train = step("train", kind="train", walltime="12h", resumable=True, produces=[model("model")])
```
