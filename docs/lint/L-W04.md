# L-W04 NoMlflowOrModelNotRegistered

**Level: warning.** A training step does not log to MLflow, or no step registers a model.

## What it checks

A GPU step that reads the `train` section (or has `train` in its name) has no MLflow in its code, or no step in the pipeline registers a model.

## Why it matters

Without logging you cannot compare runs; without a registered model the result is not kept in the registry.

## How to fix

Call `kaos_lib.mlflow.mlflow()` in the training step and log parameters and metrics; register the model you keep (`mlflow.register_model`).

## Limits of the check

It is a heuristic: it searches the step folder's code for MLflow and model registration. A pipeline that does it in an unusual way may be reported;
the check never fails the status.
