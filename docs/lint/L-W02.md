# L-W02 IdleTimeoutOff

**Level: warning.** The idle limit is turned off for a GPU step.

## What it checks

A GPU step sets `idle_timeout=0` or `False`. By default a step with no GPU use and no new log line for 20 minutes is ended.

## Why it matters

Without it a hung step keeps its GPU and your budget until the time limit.

## How to fix

Remove `idle_timeout=0`, or raise the window instead: `idle_timeout="40m"`.
