# L-I02 LearnedLimits

**Level: info.** A short limit may be shortened further by the platform.

## What it checks

A GPU step has a `hpc_time_limit` under 2 hours. After a few runs the platform may ask the site for a shorter limit, to fit the step in sooner.
A limit of 2 hours or more is kept exactly as you set it.

## How to use it

Nothing to fix. Set the limit you need.
