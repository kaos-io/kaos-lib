# L-D03 WrongArchitecture

**Level: error.** A `FROM` line asks for a CPU architecture the platform does not build for.

## What it checks

`FROM --platform=linux/arm64 ...` (or any literal platform other than `linux/amd64`). The platform builds step images for `linux/amd64` unless your project says otherwise. `--platform=$BUILDPLATFORM` and `$TARGETPLATFORM` are not judged.

## Why it matters

An image for another architecture builds, then fails on the nodes with `exec format error`. Pinning the platform in `FROM` is also what makes a local build on an Apple Silicon Mac produce an image the platform cannot run.

## How to fix

Remove `--platform=...` from `FROM`. `kaosctl build` and the platform pick the right architecture themselves.
