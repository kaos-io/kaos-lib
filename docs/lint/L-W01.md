# L-W01 LongGpuStepNotResumable

**Level: warning.** A long GPU step cannot continue after an interruption.

## What it checks

A GPU step has a `hpc_time_limit` over 4 hours and is not resumable (not declared, and no checkpoint use in its code).

## Why it matters

Machines get lost and drained. A resumable step continues from its last checkpoint; any other step starts again from zero, so
long runs can lose hours of work.

## Example

```python
train = step("train", gpu=True, hpc_time_limit="12h")
```

## How to fix

Add `resumable=True` and save checkpoints (see [Long runs on HPC (Slurm)](../checkpoint.md)), or shorten the limit.
